Who we are

Born in prayer.
Built by faith.

Awakening Saints is a prophetic ministry that began in 2017 when a family answered God’s call to establish a prayer altar.

What started from house to house later gathered in a salon, on the street and then in a dedicated room. Through prayer and the guidance of the Holy Spirit, that altar grew into a registered church and a fivefold ministry of apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers.

Today, we continue to welcome people seeking biblical truth, spiritual growth, prayer and a community in which they can discover and use their God-given gifts.

What guides us

A clear vision. A faithful mission.

Everything we do is shaped by a desire to restore biblical foundations and equip believers for a whole, purposeful life.

Our vision

To take the church back to its true foundation, teaching the true Word so believers may live fully for God on earth and in eternity.

Our mission

To turn seekers into saints, members into ministers and churchgoers into a prepared, compassionate army of God.

Our commitment

To empower people spiritually, financially, physically, morally and academically through wisdom, knowledge and understanding.
